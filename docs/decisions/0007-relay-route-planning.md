# ADR-0007: nearest-neighbour + 2-opt relay routing, not a general solver

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-09-08, Phase 2)
- **Context:** The pitch promises AI route optimization via the two-relay model: short farm-gate consolidation hops into village-level FPO hubs, then long bulk relays to urban dark stores. Routes must be demoable, deterministic, and explainable.
- **Decision:** `packages/logistics` implements: (1) each pickup order is assigned to its nearest FPO hub (haversine reused from `@farmit/pricing` — this is why `logistics` depends on `pricing`); (2) per hub, orders are capacity-batched (default 1200 kg/vehicle) into pickup tours built by nearest-neighbour and improved by 2-opt; (3) one line-haul per hub to the destination dark store. The headline metric compares consolidated distance against the one-truck-per-farmer baseline (each farm → destination → back).
- **Consequences:** Deterministic plans; every route is verifiable on paper; ~50% food-miles saved on the demo batch. 2-opt is O(n²) per improvement pass — fine at demo scale, acceptable to low thousands of stops; beyond that, swap `planRelayRoutes` internals for OR-Tools without changing the API. The baseline metric is deliberately conservative-to-favourable documented: it counts individual round trips vs. consolidated tours + shared line-hauls.
- **Alternatives rejected:** OR-Tools VRP solver now (native dependency, overkill for ≤10 stops/route); GIS service (Google/Azure routing APIs — cost + external dependency); single-stage routing (ignores the FPO-hub consolidation that is the deck's core mitigation).
